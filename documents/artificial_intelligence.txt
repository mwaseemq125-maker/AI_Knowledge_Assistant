Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Artificial intelligence is a field of computing focused on building systems that perform tasks commonly associated with human intelligence. Examples include understanding language, recognizing images, planning, and solving problems. AI systems can be rule-based or learn patterns from data.

AI applications include document analysis, translation, recommendation systems, robotics, accessibility tools, and decision support. AI output can be incorrect or incomplete, so important claims should be checked against reliable evidence. A language model generates responses from learned patterns and the context it receives; it does not automatically know whether every generated statement is true.

Responsible AI development considers privacy, fairness, security, transparency, and human oversight. A knowledge assistant can reduce the effort required to find information in a collection of documents, but its answers should remain grounded in retrieved source text.
